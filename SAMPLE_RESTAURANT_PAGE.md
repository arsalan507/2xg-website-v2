## Content Highlights

### Why This Works

1. **Immediate Relevance**
   - Headlines speak restaurant owner's language
   - Metrics focus on footfall and repeat visits (not vanity)
   - Examples are industry-specific (Reels, Google Maps, Zomato)

2. **Credible Insights**
   - "Peak sales depend on repeat visibility, not discounts"
   - "Reels outperform static food photos by 3–4x"
   - These sound like advisor experience, not marketing fluff

3. **Clear Differentiation**
   - Side-by-side comparison shows what's wrong with common approaches
   - 2XG approach is specific and strategic
   - No generic "we do content marketing" language

4. **Business-Relevant Metrics**
   - Repeat visits (not impressions)
   - Google search volume (not followers)
   - Weekend footfall (not engagement rate)

5. **Mapped Services**
   - "Content Marketing" becomes "High-frequency Reels designed to trigger cravings"
   - Shows understanding, not just listing capabilities

6. **Consultative CTA**
   - "If You're Serious About Scaling..." (qualifying language)
   - "Strategy first" (not pushy)
   - Trust signals: no contracts, data-driven

## Mobile Experience

### Key Adaptations

1. **Hero**
   - Text scales down: 7xl → 4xl
   - Buttons stack vertically on mobile

2. **Insights Grid**
   - 2 columns → 1 column on mobile
   - Cards remain full-width for readability

3. **Framework Steps**
   - Comparison splits: side-by-side → stacked
   - Content remains fully readable

4. **Metrics**
   - 3 columns → 2 columns → 1 column (responsive breakpoints)
   - Cards scale proportionally

5. **Services**
   - Arrows disappear on mobile
   - Focus on vertical flow
