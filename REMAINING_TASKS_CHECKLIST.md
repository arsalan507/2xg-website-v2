## 📞 Questions to Answer for Each Industry

Before writing content, answer these:

1. **What's the #1 pain point for this business?**
2. **What do most businesses in this space do wrong?**
3. **What metric actually matters for revenue?**
4. **What platform/channel drives the most results?**
5. **What's the typical customer journey?**
6. **What builds trust in this category?**
7. **What's the competitive landscape like?**
8. **What's unique about selling this product/service?**
